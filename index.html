Game Rules & Hand Guides

How each game works at NOIR

Automated Casino Dealer

The dealer in NOIR is completely automated by the platform engine. No player ever acts as or joins as the dealer. In Texas Hold'em, the BTN badge indicates betting order rotation (Dealer Button), while cards are dealt automatically.

Blackjack (21) Pays 3:2

Beat the dealer's card total without exceeding 21. Dealer stands on all 17s. Aces count as 1 or 11. Split any pair of equal ranks, or double your bet on the initial two cards.

Texas Hold'em No-Limit Poker

Each player receives 2 private hole cards. Combine them with the 5 shared community cards (Flop, Turn, River) to forge the best 5-card poker hand. Standard poker rankings apply (Royal Flush to High Card).

Bluff (Cheat / I Doubt It) Deception

Lead player plays 1 to 4 cards face-down and declares a rank. Subsequent players must add cards claiming the same rank or Pass. Any player can Call Bluff before the next move. If caught, the bluffer takes the entire pile; if truthful, the challenger takes it! First player to shed all cards wins.
